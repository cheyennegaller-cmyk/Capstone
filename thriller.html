Death Note

  • Description: Death Note follows highschool student Light Yagami who finds a mysterious notebook that allows him to kill anyone whose name he writes in it. He decides to use the notebook to rid the world of criminals, but his actions attract the attention of a secretive detective known only as "L". Tomadachi Game

  • Description: Tomadachi Game is a psychological thriller anime that follows the story of a high school student who becomes involved in a deadly game with his classmates. The show explores themes of friendship, betrayal, and survival as the characters are forced to compete in a series of dangerous challenges.
  • Main Characters: Yuuichi Katagiri, Tenji Mikasa, Shiho Sawaragi, Makoto Shibe, Yutori Kokorogi
